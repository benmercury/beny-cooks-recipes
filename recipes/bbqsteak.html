BBQ steak recipe

How to make the perfect BBQ steak

Ingredients

  • A good cut like sirloin or ribeye
  • Oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic or onion powder

Steps

  1. Prep: Pat steak dry. Apply oil, then generously season with your rub
  2. Preheat: Get your grill gas or charcoal very hot medium-high to high and oil the grates
  3. Grill: Sear steaks for 3-5 minutes per side for a good crust, then cook to your preferred doneness
  4. Rest: Remove steak, tent loosely with foil, and let rest for 5-10 minutes to let juices redistribute.
  5. Serve: Slice against the grain and enjoy
