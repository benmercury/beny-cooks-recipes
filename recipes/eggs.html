Scrambled eggs recipe

A guide on how to make perfect scrambled eggs

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • Butter or olive oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Whisk your egg mixture really well. Use a fork or whisk to whisk your eggs until smooth
  2. Pre-heat your pan then spread the butter or olive oil on it
  3. Add your egg mixture to the pan, preferrably a non-stick pan
  4. Using a spatula start swirling in circles to making long sweeps across the pan, making larger, creamy soft curds
  5. Finish your eggs off the heat giving the eggs a few seconds in the hot pan (off the heat)
